The final 30 days before board exams are meant for consolidation, mock testing, and quick revisions.

## 30-Day Master Plan
- **Days 1-10**: Full NCERT revision + formula sheet review.
- **Days 11-20**: Solving last 10 years' Previous Year Questions (PYQs).
- **Days 21-27**: Timed 3-hour mock exams under exact board conditions.
- **Days 28-30**: Light revision, key diagrams, formula review, and rest.
