Scoring 95+ in Class 10 CBSE Board Maths requires systematic preparation and precise step-by-step answer presentation.

## High-Weightage Chapter Groups
- **Algebra**: Polynomials, Pair of Linear Equations, Quadratic Equations, AP (20 Marks)
- **Geometry**: Triangles & Circles theorems (15 Marks)
- **Trigonometry**: Introduction & Applications (12 Marks)
- **Mensuration**: Surface Areas & Volumes (10 Marks)
- **Statistics & Probability**: High scoring with clean steps (11 Marks)

## Presentation Rules for Board Examiners
- Write given values, formula used, and final answer with units in a box.
- Draw neat geometric figures using pencil and ruler.
- Always double-check calculations in rough column on the right side.
