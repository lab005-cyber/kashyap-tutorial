Group coaching works for many students, but a large number of children thrive much better with **personalised home tuition**. Here's why.

## 1. Complete Personal Attention

In a batch of 20, a shy student may never ask a question. At home, every doubt is the tutor's immediate priority. This alone transforms understanding.

## 2. Customised Pace

Some students grasp algebra in a day, others need a week. Home tuition moves at *your* child's pace — no rushing ahead, no falling behind.

## 3. Flexible Schedule

No commuting, no fixed batch timing. Sessions can be scheduled around school, sports, and family events.

## 4. Direct Parent–Tutor Feedback

You get a daily or weekly update on:

- What was taught
- Where the child is struggling
- Homework and next steps

This transparency is hard to match in group coaching.

## 5. Comfortable Learning Environment

Children learn best where they feel safe and relaxed. Home eliminates peer pressure and distraction, letting focus do its work.

## When is Home Tuition the Right Choice?

- Your child is shy or introverted
- They've fallen behind and need to catch up
- Schedule conflicts make coaching impossible
- You want subject-specific expert help (e.g. only Physics)

## Combine Both for Best Results

Many families use **group coaching for concepts + home tuition for doubt-solving**. At Kashyap Tutorial, we offer both, so you can mix and match what works.
