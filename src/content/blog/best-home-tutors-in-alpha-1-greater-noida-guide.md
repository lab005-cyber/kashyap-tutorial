Finding a reliable, qualified home tutor in Alpha 1, Beta 1, or Knowledge Park Greater Noida requires careful evaluation.

## What to Look for in a Home Tutor
1. **Background & Qualification Verification**: Ensure the tutor has documented teaching credentials.
2. **Subject Expertise**: The tutor should specialize in the specific subject (e.g. Maths, Physics, Accounts) rather than teaching everything generically.
3. **Structured Test System**: A good home tutor conducts weekly chapter tests and updates parents regularly.
4. **Demo Class**: Always request a 1-on-1 demo session to gauge rapport and communication.
