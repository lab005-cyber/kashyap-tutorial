Achieving 95%+ in Class 12 Board Exams requires consistency, NCERT mastery, and strategic answer presentation.

## Stream-Wise Tips

### Science (PCM / PCB)
- Practice numerical problems in Physics & Physical Chemistry daily.
- Draw labeled diagrams in Biology & Physics.
- Memorize NCERT definitions and named reactions accurately.

### Commerce (Accounts, BST, Eco)
- Master balance sheet formats and working notes in Accountancy.
- Use bullet points and flowcharts in Business Studies answers.
- Practice graph diagrams and numerical formulas in Economics.
