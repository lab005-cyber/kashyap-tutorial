Choosing between CBSE (Central Board of Secondary Education) and ICSE (Indian Certificate of Secondary Education) is a crucial decision for parents.

## Key Differences

### Syllabus & Depth
- **CBSE**: Aligned directly with national entrance exams like IIT-JEE, NEET, and CUET. Focuses heavily on Science and Mathematics.
- **ICSE**: Detailed, comprehensive syllabus with strong emphasis on English Literature, Languages, Humanities, and Practical Applications.

### Grading & Assessment
- **CBSE**: Focuses on objective and application-based questions.
- **ICSE**: Emphasizes detailed answers, internal assessments, and project work.
