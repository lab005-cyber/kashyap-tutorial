Managing school hours, tuition classes, self-study, and personal rest can feel overwhelming without a structured timetable.

## The Ideal 24-Hour Student Routine
- **Morning (6:00 AM - 7:00 AM)**: 1 hour formula revision & mental recall.
- **School Hours (8:00 AM - 2:00 PM)**: Active classroom participation & note-taking.
- **Afternoon (3:00 PM - 5:00 PM)**: Tuition classes / Concept learning.
- **Evening (6:00 PM - 9:00 PM)**: Self-study, solving numericals & writing practice.
- **Night (10:00 PM)**: Sleep for at least 7 hours for memory consolidation.

Smart time management turns preparation into peak performance!
