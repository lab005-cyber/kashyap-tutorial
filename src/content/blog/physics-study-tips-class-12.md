Class 12 Physics is notorious — but it becomes manageable once you separate the subject into its two core demands: **concepts and numericals**.

## Understand, Don't Memorise

Physics is a story of *why*, not just *what*. Before solving any numerical, ask yourself:

- What physical law is at work here?
- What assumptions am I making?
- What are the units telling me?

## Derivations Are Free Marks

Board exams carry 15–20 marks in derivations. Make a dedicated notebook and learn them in your own words. Practice writing them out fully — don't just read.

## The Numerical Approach

Use this 4-step method for every problem:

1. **Read twice**, underline the given quantities
2. **Write down** what's given and what's to find
3. **Choose the right formula** (make a formula sheet per chapter)
4. **Solve with units**, then sanity-check the answer

## Chapter Priority Order

If time is short, prioritise high-weightage chapters:

- Electromagnetic Induction & AC
- Optics (huge scoring potential)
- Dual Nature & Semiconductor (easier, reliable marks)
- Current Electricity (foundational)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Ignoring units (loses easy marks)
- Skipping diagrams in optics/EMI questions
- Rote-learning formulas without understanding limits

## Practice Strategy

Solve NCERT fully, then move to previous 10-year papers. Time yourself. Most students underperform not due to knowledge, but due to **speed and silly errors**.

## Final Tip

Physics rewards the patient. Spend 15 minutes daily on a single tough problem rather than skimming 30 easy ones. Depth beats breadth here.
