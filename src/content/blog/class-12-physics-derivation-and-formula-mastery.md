Physics in Class 12 carries 70 marks in theory board exams. Mastering key derivations and formula applications is essential to score 95%+.

## 1. Top High-Yield Derivations
- **Electrostatics & Magnetism**: Gauss Law applications (line charge, plane sheet, spherical shell), Biot-Savart Law & Ampere's Circuital Law.
- **Optics**: Mirror Formula, Lens Maker Formula, Prism Refraction, Astronomical Telescope & Compound Microscope ray diagrams.
- **Electromagnetic Induction & AC**: Self & Mutual Inductance, LCR Series Circuit Resonance, Transformer efficiency.
- **Modern Physics**: Photoelectric equation, Bohr atom radius & energy levels, Radioactive decay law.

## 2. Tips for Board Presentation
- Always state initial assumptions and draw neat labeled diagrams using a ruler and pencil.
- Highlight final formula boxes with SI units clearly specified.
