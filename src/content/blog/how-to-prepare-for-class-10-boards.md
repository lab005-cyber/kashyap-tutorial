Class 10 board exams are a major milestone. A clear, disciplined strategy can make the difference between an average and an outstanding score. Here is a proven plan we recommend to all our students.

## 1. Understand the Syllabus and Exam Pattern

Before opening any book, download the latest syllabus and the exam pattern from the CBSE/ICSE website. Knowing the weightage of each unit helps you prioritise high-scoring topics.

> Spend one full evening just reading the syllabus. It removes 50% of exam anxiety.

## 2. NCERT is Non-Negotiable

For CBSE especially, **80–90% of questions** come directly or indirectly from NCERT. Read every line, including examples and exercises.

- Read the chapter fully first
- Solve all in-text and exercise questions
- Make short notes of formulas and key definitions

## 3. Build a Realistic Timetable

Don't copy someone else's timetable. Build one around your energy levels:

1. Allocate more time to weak subjects
2. Include short breaks (Pomodoro works well)
3. Reserve weekends for revision and mock tests

## 4. The Power of Revision

Reading once is not enough. Use the **3-revision rule** — revise the entire syllabus at least three times before the exam. Each revision should be faster than the last.

## 5. Solve Previous Year Papers

Practising the last 10 years of question papers is the single highest-impact activity. It trains you on:

- Question patterns and repetition trends
- Time management during the 3-hour exam
- Identifying your silly mistakes

## 6. Take Care of Your Health

All the studying in the world won't help if you burn out. Sleep 7–8 hours, eat well, and take a 30-minute walk daily.
