Mathematics in IIT-JEE is often considered the rank-decider. Unlike school board exams, JEE Mathematics tests analytical thinking, multi-concept problem solving, and rapid calculation.

## 1. Focus on Core Conceptual Clarity
At Kashyap Tutorial, we emphasize **Concept First**. Topics like Calculus, Coordinate Geometry, and Algebra cannot be solved by memorizing formulas. You must understand the derivation and geometrical interpretation of every function.

## 2. Topic-Wise Weightage Breakdown
- **Calculus**: Functions, Limits, Continuity, Differentiation, Definite Integrals (30-35% weightage)
- **Algebra**: Quadratic Equations, Matrices, Complex Numbers, Probability (30% weightage)
- **Coordinate Geometry**: Straight Lines, Circles, Parabola, Ellipse, Hyperbola (25% weightage)
- **Vectors & 3D Geometry**: Highly scoring and formula-driven (10-15% weightage)

## 3. Daily Practice & Mock Analysis
Solve at least 30-40 high-level JEE questions daily. Analyze your mock test errors immediately to identify weak areas.
