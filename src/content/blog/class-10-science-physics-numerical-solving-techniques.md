Physics numericals and ray diagrams are guaranteed scoring sections in the Class 10 Science board paper.

## Key Scoring Topics in Physics
1. **Light - Reflection & Refraction**: Mirror formula, lens formula, magnification, sign convention rules.
2. **Electricity**: Ohm's Law, series & parallel resistance combinations, Joule's heating effect, power calculations.
3. **Human Eye**: Ray diagrams for Myopia, Hypermetropia, and prism dispersion.
