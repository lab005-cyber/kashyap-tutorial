Middle school (Classes 6, 7, and 8) is the most critical phase for cognitive and analytical development.

## Why Foundation Matters Early
- **Mathematical Logic**: Algebra, Mensuration, and Fractions introduced in Class 6-8 form the base for Class 9-10 Geometry & Calculus.
- **Scientific Temper**: Basic physics concepts like Force, Motion, Light, and Electricity prevent fear of Science in high school.
- **Study Habits**: Developing disciplined homework and revision habits early prevents last-minute exam stress.
