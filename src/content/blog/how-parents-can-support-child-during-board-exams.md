Parents play an indispensable role in their child's academic success and emotional well-being during board exam season.

## Parental Support Checklist
1. **Create a Quiet Study Space**: Ensure minimal noise, good lighting, and an ergonomic desk.
2. **Avoid Peer Comparison**: Every child has a unique learning pace. Focus on personal improvement.
3. **Monitor Nutrition & Sleep**: Ensure balanced meals, hydration, and 7-8 hours of sleep.
4. **Regular Teacher Communication**: Stay in touch with tuition mentors to track progress objectively.
