Maths phobia is a common challenge among Class 9 and 10 students. However, Mathematics is 100% logical and highly scoring once fundamentals are clear.

## Steps to Eliminate Maths Phobia
- **Go Back to Basics**: Re-learn foundational arithmetic, signs rules, and basic algebraic identities.
- **Never Memorize Steps**: Understand *why* a particular formula or step is used.
- **Practice 5 Problems Daily**: Start with easy textbook examples, then move to exercises.
- **Ask Doubts Immediately**: Never let an unresolved doubt accumulate for the next day.
