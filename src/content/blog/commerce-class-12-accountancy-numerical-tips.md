Accountancy in Class 12 requires conceptual precision, proper format presentation, and error-free numerical calculations.

## Key Scoring Areas in Accountancy
1. **Partnership Accounts**: Admission, Retirement, Death, and Dissolution (36 Marks)
2. **Company Accounts**: Issue of Shares & Debentures (24 Marks)
3. **Financial Statement Analysis**: Cash Flow Statement & Ratio Analysis (20 Marks)

## Tips for Zero Error
- Always draw proper debit/credit format headers.
- Cross-check working notes for goodwill valuation and revaluation accounts.
- Practice 3-hour full syllabus sample papers before boards.
