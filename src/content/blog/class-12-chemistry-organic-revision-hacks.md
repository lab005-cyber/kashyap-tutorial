Organic Chemistry carries significant weight in Class 12 Board Exams. Mastering named reactions and conversions is key to top marks.

## 4-Step Organic Chemistry Mastery
1. **Reaction Mind Maps**: Create one-page charts for Haloalkanes, Alcohols, Aldehydes, and Amines.
2. **Named Reactions List**: Memorize Reimer-Tiemann, Kolbe's, Cannizzaro, Aldol condensation, and Hoffman Bromamide reactions.
3. **Distinction Tests**: Practice tests like Tollens', Fehling's, Lucas, and Iodoform tests.
4. **Mechanism Practice**: Practice SN1, SN2, and acid-catalyzed dehydration mechanisms thoroughly.
