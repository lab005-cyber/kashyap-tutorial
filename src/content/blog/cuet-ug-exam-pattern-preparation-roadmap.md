Common University Entrance Test (CUET UG) is mandatory for admission to premier central universities like DU, BHU, JNU, and Jamia Millia.

## CUET Exam Structure
- **Section I (Languages)**: Reading comprehension, vocabulary, and grammar.
- **Section II (Domain Subjects)**: 100% based on Class 12 NCERT syllabus.
- **Section III (General Test)**: Quantitative reasoning, logical reasoning, and current affairs.

## Tips to Crack CUET
- Master Class 12 NCERT concepts thoroughly.
- Practice mock CBT (Computer Based Tests) to increase speed and accuracy.
- Focus on elimination techniques for multiple-choice questions.
