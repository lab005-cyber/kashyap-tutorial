Biology accounts for 50% of the total marks in NEET (UG). 95%+ of questions in NEET Biology come directly from NCERT Class 11 and Class 12 textbooks.

## Key Strategies for NEET Biology
1. **Line-by-Line NCERT Reading**: Every summary, caption, and diagram footnote in NCERT contains potential questions.
2. **Diagram Practice**: Practice labelling diagrams for Human Physiology, Plant Anatomy, and Reproduction.
3. **Mnemonics & Flowcharts**: Use custom mnemonics for taxonomy, Krebs cycle, and plant hormones.
4. **Mock Question Drills**: Solve 100+ MCQs daily under timed conditions.
